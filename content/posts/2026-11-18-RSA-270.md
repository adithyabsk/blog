On November 16, 2026, I completed a long running, independent factorization of `RSA-270`
(a 270-digit (895-bit) number)

One of the factors:

```text
359353359560835771039202980394651186700927320732604439802133630990767075387516847337701049401067297464438120309866365659527898117552867
```

A swarm of Grok4.7 agents ran the computation end to end on Tesla's
SoTA heterogeneous GPU compute cluster on expressly pre-emptible jenga'd compute.
The run used ~17.9 GPU-years.

Our polynomial's Murphy E was `3.5031e-10` at `Bf=1.100e+12`,
`Bg=1.374e+11`, and `area=8.590e+19`.

Congrats to [Eric Lu](https://www.mersenneforum.org/node/1125296/page3#post1130660)
who posted a factorization on November 12, 2026. My factor matches his
result.
