RSA-270

An independent factorization of RSA-270.

November 18, 2026

On November 16, 2026, I completed a long running, independent factorization of RSA-270 (a 270-digit (895-bit) number)

One of the factors:

359353359560835771039202980394651186700927320732604439802133630990767075387516847337701049401067297464438120309866365659527898117552867

A swarm of Grok4.7 agents ran the computation end to end, and optimized kernels, on Tesla's SoTA heterogeneous GPU compute cluster on expressly pre-emptible jenga'd compute. The run used ~17.9 GPU-years (compared to 30 GPU years of a run of similar bit size).

Our polynomial's Murphy E was 3.5031e-10 at Bf=1.100e+12, Bg=1.374e+11, and area=8.590e+19.

Congrats to Eric Lu who posted a factorization on November 12, 2026. My factor matches his result. Thanks also to the great work by the CADO-NFS and cuda-sieve folks. Grok expanded this work to target many GPU variants and work within the cluster's job scalability framework.

RSA-1024 keys are certainly within reach of many actors with large compute clusters.
